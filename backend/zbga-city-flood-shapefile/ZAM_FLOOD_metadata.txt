Title: Enhanced Flood Susceptibility Map of Zamboanga City
Dataset Reference Date: 2023/12/18
Dataset Reference Date Type: Creation 
Abstract: The base flood hazard map is mainly an enriched version of the MGB flood susceptibility map, as validated by field survey data. The flooding potential is computed using the weighted sum of five ordinal factors (ranges) constituting the following: elevation (m), slope (°), HAND (m), land cover, and soil type. The relative flood susceptibility levels are determined by categorizing the flood potential into five classes using the following ranges: very low (<2.2), low (2.2-3.0), moderate (3.0-3.80), high (3.8-4.60), and very high (4.6-5.0).
