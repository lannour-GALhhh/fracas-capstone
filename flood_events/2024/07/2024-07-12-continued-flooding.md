# Continued Severe Flooding, Zamboanga City

- **Date:** 12–16 July 2024
- **Location:** Zamboanga City — Ayala, San Jose Gusu, Patalon, Pasonanca, Putik, Tetuan, Vitali, Talisayan, Recodo, Labuan, Cawit, Guiwan, Sinubong
- **Cause:** Prolonged/continuous heavy rains (continuation of the LPA/ITCZ event that began 26 Jun 2024, see [[2024-06-26-san-roque-zamboanga-city]] in `../06/`)
- **Families / Persons Affected:**
  - 12 Jul 2024: ~1,400 families evacuated to higher ground
  - 16 Jul 2024: 4,000+ families affected citywide
- **Casualties:** 6 deaths (landslides and drowning)
- **Status:** Terminated (part of the same DROMIC incident thread as the June 2024 event)

## Notes

This is the deadliest and largest-scale Zamboanga City flood event found in
the 2022–2026 window (6 deaths, 4,000+ families). DROMIC's own report numbering
continues from the June 2024 incident rather than opening a new report thread,
so this folder entry exists separately only to keep month-level detail
(news coverage was more granular than the DROMIC report summaries for this
stretch).
