# Flooding Incident in Brgy. San Roque, Zamboanga City (expanded to Zamboanga City & Zamboanga Sibugay / Zamboanga Peninsula)

- **Date:** 26 June 2024, ~7:00 PM (event continued into July 2024, see [[2024-07-12-continued-flooding]] in `../07/`)
- **Location:** Initially Brgy. San Roque, Zamboanga City; expanded to 14 barangays across Zamboanga City and Zamboanga Sibugay
- **Cause:** Continuous heavy rainfall from a Low Pressure Area (LPA) embedded with the ITCZ
- **Families / Persons Affected:**
  - Initial: 350 families / 385 persons (Brgy. San Roque only)
  - As of 01 Jul 2024: 3,167 families / 14,465 persons across 14 barangays
- **Casualties:** None reported at this stage (see July continuation for later deaths)
- **Status:** Escalated / renamed as it grew — article title changed over time from "Brgy. San Roque, Zamboanga City" → "Zamboanga City and Zamboanga Sibugay" → "Zamboanga Peninsula"
- **Source:**
  - DSWD DROMIC Report #1, as of 28 June 2024, 6AM — https://reliefweb.int/report/philippines/dswd-dromic-report-1-flooding-incident-brgy-san-roque-zamboanga-city-28-june-2024-6am
  - DSWD DROMIC Report #1 (renamed), as of 01 July 2024, 6PM — https://reliefweb.int/report/philippines/dswd-dromic-report-1-flooding-incident-zamboanga-city-and-zamboanga-sibugay-01-july-2024-6pm
  - Current article (retitled "Zamboanga Peninsula"): https://dromic.dswd.gov.ph/flooding-incident-in-zamboanga-city-and-zamboanga-sibugay-26-jun-2024/

## Notes

DROMIC tracks a single incident under one living article/URL and renames it
as the affected area grows — this event started as a single-barangay report
and was retitled twice as it spread region-wide. Treat this and the July 2024
continuation as the same underlying weather event.
