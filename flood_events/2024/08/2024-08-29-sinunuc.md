# Flooding Incident in Brgy. Sinunuc, Zamboanga City

- **Date:** 29–31 August 2024
- **Location:** Zamboanga City — Brgy. Sinunuc
- **Cause:** Localized thunderstorm
- **Families / Persons Affected:** 14 families / 55 persons
- **Casualties:** None reported
- **Status:** Terminated (terminal report issued 28 January 2025)
