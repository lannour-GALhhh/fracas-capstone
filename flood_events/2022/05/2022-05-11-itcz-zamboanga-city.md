# Flooding Incident due to ITCZ in Zamboanga City

- **Date:** 11–13 May 2022
- **Location:** Zamboanga City — several barangays along riverbanks
- **Cause:** Torrential rainfall from the Intertropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ) affecting Mindanao
- **Families / Persons Affected:** Not specified in available summary
- **Casualties:** None reported
- **Status:** Terminated
- **Source:** DSWD DROMIC Report #1, as of 13 May 2022, 6PM — https://reliefweb.int/report/philippines/dswd-dromic-report-1-flooding-incident-due-itcz-zamboanga-city-13-may-2022-6pm

## Notes

Water levels rose along riverbanks, submerging several barangays. This was the
earliest Zamboanga City-specific flooding incident found in the DROMIC archive
for the 2022–2026 window.
