- **Date:** 31 January 2023, ~2:00 PM
- **Location:** Zamboanga City — several barangays
- **Cause:** Continuous heavy rains brought by localized thunderstorms
- **Families / Persons Affected:** Not specified in available summary
- **Casualties:** None reported
- **Status:** Terminated
