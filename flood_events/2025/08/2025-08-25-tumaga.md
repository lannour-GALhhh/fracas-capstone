# Flash Flood in Brgy. Tumaga, Zamboanga City

- **Date:** 25–26 August 2025
- **Location:** Zamboanga City — Brgy. Tumaga (Filinvest, Kalayaan, Ayudahan, Paraiso Homes subdivisions)
- **Cause:** Heavy rain (Southwest Monsoon / Tropical Depression "Jacinto" / LPA); a collapsed concrete perimeter fence at Villa Ignacio, Filinvest, sent floodwater surging down Ayudahan Drive
- **Families / Persons Affected:** 407 families displaced; vehicles submerged, residents trapped in homes before rescue
- **Casualties:** None reported (rescue operations led by Mayor Khymer Olaso and CDRRMO)
- **Status:** Terminated
