# Flooding Incidents in Zamboanga City

- **Date:** ~06–07 June 2025
- **Location:** Zamboanga City — Tumaga, Pasonanca, Boalan, Tugbungan, Lunzuran
- **Cause:** Southwest Monsoon / Low Pressure Area (LPA) rainfall
- **Families / Persons Affected:** 739 families / 2,374 persons; 9 evacuation centers activated
- **Casualties:** None reported
- **Status:** Terminated
- **Source:**
  - DSWD DROMIC Report #1, as of 07 June 2025, 6PM — https://dromic.dswd.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/DSWD-DROMIC-Report-1-on-the-Flooding-Incidents-in-Zamboanga-City-as-of-07-June-2025-6PM.pdf
  - PIA — Zambo City mobilizes immediate response to flood-hit barangays — https://pia.gov.ph/news/zambo-city-mobilizes-immediate-response-to-flood-hit-barangays/
  - PCO — PBBM orders swift relief for thousands of Zamboanga City flash flood victims — https://pco.gov.ph/news_releases/pbbm-orders-swift-relief-for-thousands-of-zamboanga-city-flash-flood-victims/

## Notes

President Marcos ordered DSWD to expedite relief; 571 boxes of family food
packs were distributed to Brgy. Pasonanca alone. Distinct incident from the
25–26 Aug 2025 Tumaga flash flood ([[2025-08-25-tumaga]] in `../08/`) — same
barangay (Tumaga) flooded twice in 2025 from separate weather systems.
